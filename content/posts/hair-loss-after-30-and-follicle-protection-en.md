## Biological mechanism: The encounter between testosterone and metabolic enzymes

To understand why hair sheds increasingly over time, we must look deep into the body's metabolic factory. Testosterone, a familiar hormone found in both men and women, circulates through the scalp where it encounters a catalytic protein called 5-alpha reductase (abbreviated as 5-AR). This enzyme acts like a sharp pair of scissors, converting testosterone into dihydrotestosterone, commonly known as DHT. When excess DHT accumulates around hair roots, it sends false signals that cause scalp blood vessels to constrict. Much like a water pipe clogged over time, the hair follicle is gradually choked and miniaturized, producing thinner strands that break easily long before completing their natural lifecycle.

## Practical applications and root-cause hair care strategies

Protecting your hair from age-related thinning does not require complicated or costly therapies. First, incorporate zinc-rich foods such as pumpkin seeds, oysters, and whole grains into your daily diet, as zinc is an essential element curbing excessive 5-AR activity. Additionally, utilizing supportive supplements containing standardized Saw Palmetto berry extract at proper dosages is clinically proven to protect follicles effectively. Combine adequate sleep and stress reduction with targeted nutrition to cultivate resilient and lustrous hair over time.

## 1. Molecular Dynamics: The Bioactive Dipeptide Secret

Native triple-helical collagen possesses an unwieldy molecular weight approaching 300,000 Daltons, preventing mucosal penetration. Precision enzymatic cleavage reduces this mass to low-molecular **bioactive peptides** under 3,000 Daltons.
        
Crucially, specific oligopeptide sequences including **Prolyl-hydroxyproline (Pro-Hyp)** and **Hydroxyprolyl-glycine (Hyp-Gly)** resist brush-border peptidase degradation, circulating intact to trigger tissue remodeling.

## 3. Evidence-Based Protocol & Practical Takeaways

Translational strategies to maximize connective tissue longevity: - **Verify Molecular Weight**: Select standardized hydrolysates exhibiting mean molecular mass between 2,000 and 3,000 Daltons. - **Daily Threshold**: Maintain continuous intake of 5g to 10g daily for a baseline window of at least 8 to 12 weeks. - **Synergistic Co-factor**: Ensure adequate Vitamin C co-ingestion, which serves as an obligatory co-substrate for prolyl hydroxylase in de novo cross-linking.

## 1. Molecular Pharmacokinetics: PEPT1 Transporter Uptake

Inorganic magnesium salts exhibit poor human bioavailability (rarely exceeding 4% to 10%) because unbound divalent cations draw fluid into the intestinal lumen via osmotic pressure. In contrast, **Magnesium Bisglycinate** comprises an elemental ion stably bound between two glycine molecules.
        
This organic chelate resists dietary phytate antagonism and utilizes specialized mucosal dipeptide carriers (**PEPT1**), enabling systemic bioavailability exceeding 80% without digestive distress.

## 3. Practical Protocols & Actionable Recommendations

Translational guidelines for restoring restful circadian rest: - **Timing and Dosage**: Administer 200mg - 300mg of elemental magnesium as pure bisglycinate 30 to 60 minutes prior to retiring. - **Synergistic Stacking**: Co-administration with 100mg L-Theanine produces additive calming through central GABAergic amplification. - **Safety Precaution**: Individuals with advanced renal insufficiency must monitor serum magnesium levels under medical supervision.
